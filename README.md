# 🧩 Cube100 — Relax in the World of Cubes

Welcome to **Cube100** — an interactive Rubik's Cube solver created not only for
solving puzzles, but also for providing a little peace, focus, and relaxation.

With Cube100, you can scramble, solve, and simply watch cubes of different sizes
— starting from the classic **2×2×2** and going far beyond.

## 🧘 What is it for?

Sometimes you just want to take a break, turn off the unnecessary noise around
you, and relax for a while.

That's what Cube100 is about.

Watch the faces rotate, follow the sequence of moves, and gradually transform a
completely scrambled cube into a solved one.

No rush.

No competition.

No records to beat.

Just a cube, movement, and a little time for yourself.

**Cube100 is more than just a digital puzzle solver — it's a small space for
relaxation and calm.**

## 🎲 Cube Sizes

Cube100 supports cube sizes starting from:

- 2×2×2
- 3×3×3
- 4×4×4
- 5×5×5
- 10×10×10
- 20×20×20
- 30×30×30
- 40×40×40
- 50×50×50
- and beyond

You can experiment with larger sizes and see how far your computer can go.

### 💻 Recommended Size

For a comfortable experience, a cube size of **up to 50×50×50** is recommended.

However, the actual limit depends on your computer's performance, including the
CPU, GPU, available memory, and the number of operations being performed.

If your computer can handle more — feel free to experiment.

**Cube100 does not impose a strict maximum size.**

Your computer will tell you when it's time to stop. 🙂

## 🧠 The Solver

Cube100 can automatically solve a scrambled cube by going through the solving
process step by step.

This allows you not only to get a solved cube, but also to watch the entire
solving process.

Choose a comfortable animation speed and watch as chaos gradually turns into
order.

Larger cubes naturally require more processing and visualization time, so don't
worry if a large cube takes longer to solve.

The bigger the cube, the more elements need to be processed.

## 🌌 Big Cubes

Large cube sizes are one of the main features of Cube100.

A 3×3 cube looks familiar.

A 10×10 cube makes you stop and take a closer look.

A 50×50 cube becomes a huge digital canvas made of thousands of tiny elements.

## 🎵 Music & Atmosphere

To make the experience even more relaxing, Cube100 includes background classical

You can simply let the cube solve itself while you take a moment to relax.

## ⚙️ Performance

Performance depends directly on the cube size.

As the size increases, the number of elements that need to be calculated and
rendered also increases.

Therefore:

> **The bigger the cube, the more work your computer has to do.**

If you experience delays, lower frame rates, or longer calculation times with
large cubes, try reducing the cube size.

For most computers, a range of **2×2×2 to 50×50×50** should provide a
comfortable experience.

However, this is not a strict limitation.

Experiment and find the size that feels right for your computer.
