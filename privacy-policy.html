Privacy Policy

For CubeRelax100

Last updated: November 10, 2026

CubeRelax100 is a 3D cube puzzle application. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your cube state, selected cube size, and related app settings locally on your device using browser local storage. This information is used to restore your settings and cube state when you use the app again. It is not sent by the app to the developer.

Music

The app includes audio tracks, mainly nocturnes by Frédéric Chopin, as part of its app files. These tracks are played within the app. The app does not download these music files from a server during use.

In-app purchases

CubeRelax100 offers an optional one-time Premium purchase to unlock cube sizes from 7×7 to 100×100. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. The app receives purchase-related information from Google Play to verify the purchase and provide the purchased features. The developer does not receive or process your payment card details. Payment processing is governed by Google Play's terms and privacy policy.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising and analytics

The app does not display advertisements and does not use Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics.

Information sharing

The app does not sell personal information. Information needed to process and verify an in-app purchase is handled through Google Play. The app's web content is hosted using GitHub Pages; when the app loads this content, GitHub may process technical information as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Children's privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require users to create an account.

Data security and retention

Cube state and settings remain in local storage on your device until you clear the app or browser data or remove the stored information. Purchase records are handled by Google Play according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page.
